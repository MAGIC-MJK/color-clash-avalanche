# Original Color Clash collectibles

The four card-back images and two outfit preview images in this project were generated for Color Clash with OpenAI image generation in October 2026. They are original game assets. The mountain, treasure, and crystal designs are abstract visual themes; they are not official cryptocurrency logos or endorsements.

The hanging shop sign at `../shop-sign.png` was generated for Color Clash as original art. It takes the general idea of a tactile tavern sign from the user-provided reference pack without using its extracted game texture or model.
